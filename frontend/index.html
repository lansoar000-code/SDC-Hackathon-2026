Data & models

Download CSV template ↓

Upload UTF-8 CSV or XLSX, up to 16 MB or 100,000 rows. Import your selected file before training. Duplicate date and dish combinations are rejected; an invalid row cancels the entire import.

File format & required fields

Required: date, dish_id, dish_name, sales_quantity. Optional: prepared_quantity, waste_quantity, weather, is_exam_week, has_event.

Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, non-negative integer quantities, sunny/cloudy/rainy weather and true/false flags. Missing dates are not treated as zero sales.

At least 21 saved dates are required to compare XGBoost, Ridge and the historical baseline.
